## Full description (both stores)

> La Fuerza is the private coaching app for clients of [Coach name].
>
> If your coach has given you an account, log in to get:
>
> **Your training plan** — the workouts your coach built for you, with sets, reps, target weights, and exercise video demonstrations. Log every set as you train and your coach sees your progress.
>
> **Nutrition guidance** — daily food logging with targets set by your coach, plus recipes chosen for your plan.
>
> **Progress tracking** — bodyweight, steps, and weekly check-ins with photos, so you and your coach can see how far you've come.
>
> **Direct connection to your coach** — your coach reviews your logs and check-ins and adjusts your plan as you improve.
>
> La Fuerza is available to coaching clients only. An account is created for you personally by your coach — there is no public sign-up. If you're interested in coaching, contact [Coach name] at [website/Instagram/email].
