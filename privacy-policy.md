La Fuerza ("the app") is operated by [Coach's legal name / business name] ("we", "your coach"). It is a private coaching app for the coach's clients. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

## What we collect

**Account data.** Your email address, name, and an encrypted password, stored with our backend provider Supabase. Your account is created by your coach; the app has no public sign-up.

**Profile photos.** If you choose to set a profile photo, it is uploaded to Supabase Storage. Taking a photo uses your device camera only with your permission.

**Training data.** Workout plans your coach assigns, and everything you log in the app: completed sets, reps and weights, workout sessions, bodyweight entries, step counts, food/nutrition logs, and weekly check-ins (which may include photos you choose to upload).

**Exercise videos.** Demonstration videos in the exercise library are uploaded by your coach, not collected from clients.

## What we do NOT collect
- No location data, contacts, or advertising identifiers.
- No tracking across other apps or websites; the app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

## How your data is used
Your data is used solely to deliver the coaching service: your coach sees your logs, check-ins, and progress in order to adjust your plan. Data is never sold and never shared with anyone other than you and your coach.

## Where your data lives
Data is stored by [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), our backend provider, in the [region — check your Supabase project settings] region. Transmission is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.

## How long we keep it
Your data is kept while you have an active account. Some data may also be cached on your own device so the app works offline; this cache is removed when the app is uninstalled.

## Your rights
- **Export:** In the app, go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Export Data to download a copy of your data.
- **Deletion:** In the app, go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your logs, and your uploaded photos from our systems.
- **Questions or corrections:** contact us at [support email — must match the address in the app's Help & Support screen].
