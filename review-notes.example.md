## Notes for the reviewer (paste as-is, then fill placeholders)

> La Fuerza is a private coaching app for the clients of a single personal trainer.
> It is intentionally login-gated: the coach creates each client's account personally
> as part of her paid coaching service, so there is **no public self-signup**. This is
> the intended design, not an incomplete feature — the app serves an existing closed
> coach–client relationship (similar to a gym's member app).
>
> Demo accounts (full access, pre-populated with realistic data):
>
> - Coach: `<DEMO_COACH_EMAIL>` / `<DEMO_COACH_PASSWORD>`
> - Client: `<DEMO_CLIENT_EMAIL>` / `<DEMO_CLIENT_PASSWORD>`
>
> The client account shows the typical client experience (assigned workout plan,
> set logging, nutrition, progress, check-ins). The coach account shows how client
> accounts and plans are managed.
>
> **Sign in with Apple:** not offered because the app has no third-party or social
> login of any kind (Guideline 4.8 does not apply). Authentication is email +
> password only, with accounts provisioned by the coach.
>
> **Account deletion:** available in-app at Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete
> Account (Guideline 5.1.1(v)).
>
> **User-generated content:** clients can only upload content (photos, logs) visible
> to themselves and their own coach. There is no public feed, no client-to-client
> interaction, and no content discovery, so no reporting/blocking mechanism is
> applicable.
